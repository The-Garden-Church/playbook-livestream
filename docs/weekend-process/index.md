# Weekend Process

This section covers everything that happens on a weekend service from a Livestream Team perspective. Follow the checklists in order.

## Sunday morning timeline

| Time | What's happening |
| --- | --- |
| 6:00 AM | Setup begins. Meet at the sound booth in the auditorium |
| 7:30 AM | Full team huddle behind the stage |
| 8:00 AM | Service 1 (we stream this) |
| 9:30 AM | Service 2 (we stream this) |
| Before 11:00 AM | Pack up. Service 3 starts at 11 and is not on our schedule |

The Livestream Team is on campus from 6:00 AM through pack-up after Service 2. Plan your morning around that.

## Where to go

- [Pre-Service Checklist](pre-service.md). Setup and systems check before doors open.
- [During Service](during-service.md). Roles, comms, and what runs on the board.
- [Post-Service Checklist](post-service.md). Teardown, archiving, and wrap-up.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). Common problems and how to fix them fast.

!!! warning "Live production rule"
    If something is wrong, say it out loud immediately. The Team Lead would rather know about a problem early than discover it late.
