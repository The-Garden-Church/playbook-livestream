Quick-reference for the most common problems during a live service.

---

!!! danger "Emergency in the room: people before production"
    If something puts people at risk in the room (medical event, fire alarm, security incident, evacuation), **stop the stream and help.**

    1. **Stop the stream.** A black screen is the right answer.
    2. **Help.** Get out of the way of first responders, security, or pastoral staff. If you can help someone directly, do.
    3. **Defer to security and church staff** for direction. They lead the response.
    4. **Do not start the stream back up** until the Team Lead or pastoral staff give the green light.

    Nothing on the stream is more important than the people in the room.

---

## Stream dropped / went offline

1. Check internet connection first. Run a speed test if needed
2. Check encoder software for error messages
3. Restart the stream. Reconnect cleanly
4. Notify Team Lead immediately
5. Check platform dashboard for any platform-side outages

## Video signal lost

1. Check cable connections at source and destination
2. Check switcher input routing
3. Power cycle the affected camera if needed
4. Switch to a backup camera or angle if available

---

## Audio dropout or no audio on stream

1. Confirm audio feed is active from FOH or audio interface
2. Check levels in streaming software. Confirm it is not muted and not clipping
3. Check physical cable connections
4. Restart audio driver if it is a software-level issue

---

## Graphics not advancing / frozen

1. Check that the correct file is open
2. Restart graphics software if unresponsive
3. Team Lead can manually cue slides if needed as a backup
