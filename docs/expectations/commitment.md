# Commitment

## Scheduling

The team is scheduled through **Planning Center Services**. Install the app, sign in, and turn on notifications.

- [Planning Center Services for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/planning-center-services/id509143998)
- [Planning Center Services for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ministrycentered.PlanningCenter)

### Responding to serve requests

When a serve request comes through, **respond in a reasonable timeframe.** Do not let it sit for weeks.

- Accepting is great. Declining is also fine, that is what the button is there for.
- If you need to decline, **do it with enough lead time** for the Team Lead to find someone else. Don't wait until the last minute.
- The team can plan around a "no" given two weeks out. The team cannot plan around a "no" given Saturday night.

### Keep your unavailability up to date

In Planning Center, **block out the dates you know you cannot serve** (vacations, work travel, family commitments, etc.) and **keep that list current.** This is the easiest way to avoid being scheduled for a date you cannot make, and it saves the Team Lead from chasing replacements.

### If you become unavailable after being scheduled

Once you are on the schedule, you are committed to that date. If something legitimately changes and you can no longer serve, **it is your responsibility to find a replacement.**

1. Post in the team GroupMe chat and ask if anyone can fill in for you.
2. Once someone agrees, confirm the swap with the Team Lead so the schedule is updated in Planning Center.
3. If you cannot find anyone, tell the Team Lead as early as possible so they can help cover.

## Arrival time

If you are scheduled to serve, **arrival time is 6:00 AM**. Not 6:05, not 6:15. We have a lot to set up before doors open and the team is counting on you to be there.

If something comes up and you are going to be late, **post in the team GroupMe channel** so the rest of the team knows and can adjust. A heads-up changes a problem into a manageable situation.

## Tardiness

Stuff happens once in a while, and we get it. But **regular tardiness will not be accepted**. Live production runs on trust, and the team has to know they can count on you to show up when you said you would. Repeated lateness will be addressed directly by the Team Lead and may result in being removed from the schedule.

## Attendance

Reliability is one of the most important traits on a live production team. Last-minute no-shows create real problems. We understand life happens, just communicate early.

## Communication

All team communication happens in the team **GroupMe** channel. It is the primary way the team coordinates, and staying in the loop is part of the commitment.

**Install the app and turn on notifications.** Email and SMS forwarding are not enough, you need to be in the app.

- [GroupMe for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/groupme/id392796698)
- [GroupMe for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.groupme.android)

You are expected to monitor the channel and respond in a timely manner, especially in the day or two leading up to a service you are scheduled for.

**Acknowledge posts with a reaction.** When a message goes out, drop a thumbs up or another quick reaction so the sender knows it was seen. This is especially important for posts that are team-wide or directed at you. Silence reads as "did you see this or not?" and creates extra back-and-forth. A reaction takes a second and keeps the team moving.

## Stepping off the team

Seasons change. School, work, family, health, a needed break — any of those are real reasons to step off the team for a while or for good. **There is no shame in that.** Just don't ghost.

If you need to pause or step off:

1. Talk to Jay directly. A quick GroupMe DM is enough to start the conversation.
2. Give as much notice as you can so the schedule can be adjusted.
3. Finish out anything you're already scheduled for, or help find a replacement.

!!! quote "The door stays open"
    If life shifts again and you want to come back, you're welcome.
