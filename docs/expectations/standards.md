# Standards & Culture

## Before you arrive

- Get enough sleep. Live production requires focus.
- Review the run of show if one is available.
- Arrive at call time, not service time.

## During the service

- No phones out during service unless it's part of your role.
- Communicate problems immediately — don't sit on an issue.
- If you don't know, ask. Guessing on live production is how things go wrong.

## After the service

- Don't leave until teardown is complete.
- Flag any gear issues to the Team Lead before you go.
- Debrief honestly — what went well, what didn't.

## General

We serve the church with excellence. That means caring about the details, being on time, and treating the gear and the space with respect.
