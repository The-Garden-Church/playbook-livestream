# Join the Livestream Team

The Livestream Team makes it possible for people to experience The Garden Church from anywhere in the world. We handle everything from camera operation to live encoding to stream monitoring — and we're always looking for people who want to serve in this way.

## Who we're looking for

You don't need a broadcast degree. You need to be reliable, teachable, and care about doing things well.

Good fits include people who:

- Enjoy technology and are comfortable learning new software
- Can commit to a consistent weekend schedule
- Work well under pressure and communicate clearly
- Want to use their skills to serve the church

## What you'll be doing

Livestream Team Members serve in specific roles each weekend. Depending on your role, that might include operating cameras, managing the stream encoder, monitoring audio/video output, or running graphics.

Every role is taught — no experience required to start.
