Team Members serve on a rotating schedule. A typical serve includes:

- Arriving before the service for setup and systems check
- Serving through the full service
- Staying briefly after for teardown and debrief

## The learning curve

Most new team members shadow an experienced volunteer for their first few serves before taking on a role independently. The Team Lead will work with you to find the right starting point based on your background.

## Culture

We take the work seriously without taking ourselves too seriously. We're a team, not a crew of specialists who operate in silos. Everyone helps, everyone communicates, and everyone cleans up.
