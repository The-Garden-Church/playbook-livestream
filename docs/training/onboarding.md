# Onboarding

Welcome to the Livestream Team.

This page walks you through your first few weeks. The [Expectations](../expectations.md) page is the long-term reference for how the team operates day to day. This page is the **starter path**: what to set up, what your first serves will look like, and what to bring with you on day one.

---

## Step 1: Get set up before your first serve

A handful of one-time things to knock out as soon as you're on the team. Most of it takes about 20 minutes.

- [ ] **Get added to the team GroupMe.** Ask the Team Lead or another team member to add you. This is where the team coordinates. Install the app and turn on notifications.
- [ ] **Install Planning Center Services and sign in.** This is where you'll see your serve requests and your schedule. Turn on notifications so you don't miss a request.
- [ ] **Add a real profile photo to GroupMe and Planning Center.** Use a clear picture of **you**, not a pet, sunset, or logo. Faces help the team coordinate quickly and welcome new people.
- [ ] **Add your unavailability in Planning Center.** Vacations, work trips, anything you already know about. Set this up early so you don't get scheduled for dates you can't make.
- [ ] **Pick up your Livestream badge from the Team Lead.** You'll wear this any time you're serving.
- [ ] **Read through the [Expectations](../expectations.md) page.** This is what the team is committed to. Read it once now so nothing is a surprise later.
- [ ] **Skim the [Weekend Process](../weekend-process/index.md).** You don't need to memorize it. Know the rhythm of a serve so it feels familiar when you show up.
- [ ] **Browse the [Glossary](glossary.md).** A short reference for the production terms the team uses. Skim it so you know it exists.

## Your role

!!! info "One role for now: Livestream Tech"
    Everyone trains into this role first. As the team grows, we'll add specialized roles (camera, audio, graphics, etc.) and you'll have the chance to grow into one of them.

The team password vault lives on the **Livestream laptop**, so you don't need to install anything on your personal device. The Team Lead will walk you through accessing it during your first serve.

If anything is blocked or unclear, message the Team Lead in GroupMe.

---

## Step 2: Getting hands-on

You'll be in real serving from day one. You will not be the one **responsible** for a role yet, but you also won't be sitting in the corner watching.

New Team Members work alongside experienced ones. You'll be doing real work, helping run the service, picking things up as you go. The experienced Team Member is on point for the role; you're in it with them.

There is no fixed number of "training serves" before you take a role independently. The Team Lead will tell you when you're ready. You can always ask for another serve alongside someone if you'd rather feel solid than rushed.

Ask questions. Try things. The team would rather walk you through something twice than have you guess on it live.

## What to expect on day one

A quick cheat sheet for your first morning. The full Sunday timeline lives on the [Weekend Process](../weekend-process/index.md) page. Read it before your first serve.

### Getting there

- **Park away from the doors.** The close spots are for attendees, especially first-time guests and families. We park further out.
- **Meet at the sound booth in the auditorium at 6:00 AM.** Two team bins with all our gear will be there waiting.
- **Coffee is in the lobby.** Grab some on your way in.

### How to show up

- **Wear dark or black clothes**, modest, pants or jeans (not shorts).
- **Wear your Livestream badge** any time you're serving.
- **Bring water and something to eat if you need to.** Keep it tucked away at your station, not out on the gear.
- **Phones stay pocketed** during service unless your role uses one.
- **Find the Team Lead or your shadow partner** when you arrive so you know where to plug in.

You will not be expected to know everything. You **will** be expected to show up, pay attention, and ask questions when you don't know something.
