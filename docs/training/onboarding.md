# Onboarding

Welcome to the team. Here's how your first few serves will go.

## Serve 1 — Shadow

You'll shadow an experienced Team Member in a role we think fits your background. Your job is to watch and ask questions. You won't be running anything independently yet.

## Serve 2 — Assisted

You'll take the role with an experienced Team Member alongside you. They'll guide you through the checklist and be available the whole time.

## Serve 3 — Independent (with check-ins)

You'll run the role on your own. The Team Lead will check in before and after. Most people are ready for this by serve 3, but we'll adjust the timeline based on how you're feeling.

## What to do before your first serve

- [ ] Read through the [Expectations](../expectations/index.md) section
- [ ] Review the [Weekend Process](../weekend-process/index.md) overview
- [ ] Get access to the team communication channel
- [ ] Get access to the team password vault

## Questions

Ask the Team Lead anything before, during, or after your first serve. There are no dumb questions in training.
