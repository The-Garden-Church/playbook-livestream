# Onboarding

Welcome to the Livestream Team. We're glad you're here.

This page walks you through your first few weeks. The [Expectations](../expectations/index.md) section is the long-term reference for how the team operates day in and day out. This page is the **starter path**: what to set up, what your first serves will look like, and what to bring with you on day one.

---

## Step 1 — Get set up before your first serve

A handful of one-time things to knock out as soon as you're on the team. Most of this can be done from your couch in 20 minutes.

- [ ] **Get added to the team GroupMe.** Ask Jay or another team member to add you. This is where the team actually talks. Once you're in, install the app and turn on notifications.
- [ ] **Install Planning Center Services and sign in.** This is where you'll see your serve requests and your schedule. Turn on notifications so you don't miss a request.
- [ ] **Add a real profile photo to GroupMe and Planning Center.** A clear picture of **you** — not your dog, not a sunset, not a logo. Putting a face to a name matters when the team is coordinating fast or scheduling new people.
- [ ] **Add your unavailability in Planning Center.** Vacations, work trips, anything you already know about. Set this up early so you don't get scheduled for dates you can't make.
- [ ] **Pick up your Livestream badge from Jay.** You'll wear this any time you're serving.
- [ ] **Read through the [Expectations](../expectations/index.md) section.** This is what the team is committed to. Read it once now so nothing is a surprise later.
- [ ] **Skim the [Weekend Process](../weekend-process/index.md).** You don't need to memorize it. Just know the rhythm of a serve so it feels familiar when you show up.
- [ ] **Browse the [Glossary](glossary.md).** A short reference for the production terms you'll hear thrown around. You don't need to memorize it, just know it exists.

## Your role

!!! info "One role for now: Livestream Tech"
    Everyone trains into this role first. As the team grows, we'll add specialized roles (camera, audio, graphics, etc.) and you'll have the chance to grow into one of them.

The team password vault lives on the **Livestream laptop**, so you don't need to install anything on your personal device. Jay will walk you through accessing it during your first serve.

If anything is blocked or unclear, message Jay in GroupMe.

## Step 2 — Your first three serves

We don't throw anyone in the deep end. Your first three serves are designed to take the pressure off so you can actually learn.

### Serve 1 — Shadow

You'll shadow an experienced Team Member in a role we think fits your background. Your job is to **watch and ask questions.** You won't be running anything independently yet. Bring a notebook or just pay attention, whatever helps you learn.

### Serve 2 — Assisted

You'll take the role, with an experienced Team Member alongside you. They'll walk you through the checklist live and be there to catch anything that gets missed. The goal is to start feeling the timing of a real service.

### Serve 3 — Independent (with check-ins)

You'll run the role on your own. Jay will check in with you before service starts and after teardown. Most people are ready for this by serve 3, but if you want another assisted serve, just say so. We'd rather you feel solid than rushed.

## What to expect on day one

A quick cheat sheet so your first morning isn't stressful. The full Sunday timeline lives on the [Weekend Process](../weekend-process/index.md) page — read it before your first serve.

### Getting there

- **Park away from the doors.** The close spots are for attendees, especially first-time guests and families. We park further out.
- **Meet at the sound booth in the auditorium at 6:00 AM.** Two team bins with all our gear will be there waiting.
- **Coffee is in the lobby**, grab some on your way in.

### How to show up

- **Wear dark or black clothes**, modest, pants or jeans (not shorts).
- **Wear your Livestream badge** any time you're serving.
- **Bring water and something to eat if you need to** — but keep it tucked away at your station, not out on the gear.
- **Phones stay pocketed** during service unless your role uses one.
- **Find Jay or your shadow partner** when you arrive so you know where to plug in.

You will not be expected to know everything. You **will** be expected to show up, pay attention, and ask questions when you don't know something.

Ask Jay anything, before, during, or after your first serve. There are no dumb questions in training, and the team would rather answer the same thing twice than have you guess on something live.

When in doubt, message in GroupMe. That's what it's there for.
