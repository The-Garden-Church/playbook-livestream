# Practice: dial in the shot

This is a practice camera. It shows what each setting does to the picture, so you can learn to spot a wrong setting and fix it before the director has to ask.

The controls run in the same order as [Dialing in your camera](dial-in.md): shutter, white balance, tint, aperture, ISO, then focus. The room light is 4000 K and the standard settings are f/4, 180°, ISO 400 and 4000 K with no tint.

## How to use it

- **Free play.** Move one control at a time and watch what changes. Try f/2 and f/16, ISO 6400, or a white balance of 2500 K and 8000 K.
- **Challenges.** Each one starts with something wrong, like a real Sunday. Read what the director noticed, fix it, then press **Director ready?** to check your work.
- **Camera tools.** Turn on zebras, false color, focus peaking or the histogram to measure the picture instead of trusting your eyes.

!!! info "It's a simulation"
    The picture is a drawing of a stage, not a real camera feed. The settings behave like a real camera, so the habits carry over.
