# Join the Team

The Livestream Team makes it possible for people to experience The Garden Church from anywhere in the world. We handle camera operation, live encoding, and stream monitoring, and we're looking for people who want to serve in this way.

!!! note "Team Members, not volunteers"
    On this team, you are a **Team Member**, not a volunteer. The work is real, the commitment is real, and the contribution is real. You are part of the team, not helping the team out.

## Who we're looking for

You don't need a broadcast degree. You need to be reliable, teachable, and care about doing things well.

Good fits include people who:

- Enjoy technology and are comfortable learning new software
- Can commit to a consistent weekend schedule
- Work well under pressure and communicate clearly
- Want to use their skills to serve the church

## What you'll be doing

Livestream Team Members serve in specific roles each weekend. Depending on your role, that might include operating cameras, managing the stream encoder, monitoring audio/video output, or running graphics.

Every role is taught. No experience required to start.

Team Members serve on a rotating schedule. A typical serve includes:

- Arriving before the service for setup and systems check
- Serving through the full service
- Staying briefly after for teardown and debrief

Most new team members shadow an experienced volunteer for their first few serves before taking on a role independently. The Team Lead will work with you to find the right starting point based on your background.

We take the work seriously. We work as one team, not a set of specialists in silos. Everyone helps, everyone communicates, and everyone cleans up.

The best way to join the team is to reach out directly to the Team Lead or fill out the volunteer interest form on the church website.

From there:

1. We'll schedule a quick conversation to learn about your background and interest
2. You'll be invited to shadow a weekend service
3. If it's a good fit, we'll get you on the schedule

Questions? Reach out to the Team Lead directly.
