## Talk to the Team Lead

The Team Lead is available for the team. The best way to reach them is through the team **GroupMe** app.

- Drop a message in the team GroupMe chat for anything the whole team should see
- Direct message the Team Lead in GroupMe for anything one-on-one
- Need a longer conversation or a 1:1? Ask the Team Lead in GroupMe and they will set up a time

If you are not in the team GroupMe yet, ask the Team Lead or another team member to add you.
