## Talk to Jay

Jay is the Team Lead and is available for the team. The best way to reach him is through the team **GroupMe** app.

- Drop a message in the team GroupMe chat for anything the whole team should see
- Direct message Jay in GroupMe for anything one-on-one
- Need a longer conversation or a 1:1? Ask Jay in GroupMe and he will set up a time

If you are not in the team GroupMe yet, ask Jay or another team member to add you.
